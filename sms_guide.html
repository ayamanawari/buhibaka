SMS Commands Guide

Available Two-Way SMS Command
STATUS

Check Status

Replies with real-time Water %, Feed %, and Turbidity status.

REFILL

Manual Refill

Triggers manual water refill (activates solenoid valve).

FEED

Manual Feed

Triggers manual feed dispense (activates Feed Motor).

STOP

Emergency Stop

Immediately stops active refill or dispense operations.

AUTO ON

Enable Automation

Enables automatic sensor-based threshold refilling.

AUTO OFF

Disable Automation

Disables automatic refilling (manual / SMS mode only).

Automated SMS Alert Notifications

Low Water Alert

"ALERT: Water level low (30%). Automatic refill started."

Low Feed Alert

"ALERT: Feed level low (30%). Automatic dispense started."

Dirty Water Alert

"WARNING: High water turbidity detected. Please check trough."
